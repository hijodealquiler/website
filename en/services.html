Older adults

Company and support to keep living your life.

No longer driving, or simply prefer not to go alone. We accompany you with respect and calm, at your own pace, for your daily errands and outings.

We pick you up at home and accompany you wherever you need to go: the doctor, the pharmacy, the bank, the shops, a family visit, or even church. It's not just a ride, we stay with you through the whole activity.

  • Door to door — pick-up and return home
  • Company inside — appointment, shop, church or event
  • Always the same person — a familiar face, not a different stranger each time
  • At your pace — no rushing

Medical appointments

They should never go to an appointment alone.

Pick-up at home, companionship inside the appointment, and a safe return. We message you by WhatsApp at every step so you can relax, wherever you are.

  • Before — punctual pick-up at home
  • During — we wait with them in the room, and help make sense of instructions if needed
  • After — return home, with a WhatsApp update to the family

Ideal for specialists, tests, check-ups, physiotherapy, oncology or dialysis: any appointment where the wait is long and company matters.

Hospital discharge

The trip home, accompanied.

After a hospital stay or surgery, we pick your family member up from hospital and stay with them until they're safely settled at home.

  • Hospital pick-up — at the time the discharge is confirmed
  • A calm journey — no rushing, with attention to how they're feeling
  • Help settling in at home — getting comfortable, picking up prescriptions on the way if needed
  • Family update — by WhatsApp as soon as they're home

Note: our vehicle is not wheelchair-adapted; we cover discharges where the person can walk with support.

Shopping and errands

The weekly shop, without carrying it alone.

We accompany you to the supermarket, pharmacy, bank, or any administrative errand, helping carry the bags and get things done at a calm pace.

  • Supermarket and market — we come inside with you, and carry the bags
  • Bank and admin — errands that can be hard to manage alone
  • Pharmacy — picking up prescriptions and medication
  • Back home — with everything put away

Temporary reduced mobility

While you recover, you don't have to go it alone.

After surgery, a fall, or a period of recovery, we offer the same close, reliable companionship for those walking with support.

Designed for people walking with a cane, a walker, or another person's support, but who don't need a wheelchair. We accompany you to check-ups, rehab, or errands while you regain your usual mobility.

Important: our vehicle is not wheelchair-adapted. If your family member needs one, get in touch and we'll point you toward an adapted service.

Long distance

Madrid, Barcelona, or wherever they need to be.

Long-distance trips with the same close companionship: to see a specialist, visit family, or any occasion worth the journey.

  • Same-day round trips — for specific appointments
  • With overnight stay — if the trip requires it
  • Comfortable stops — at your pace, not ours
  • One single point of contact — before, during and after the trip

Ask about rates for Madrid, Barcelona or other destinations across the Comunidad Valenciana on WhatsApp.

Busy sons and daughters

We go in your place.

If you live outside Valencia, or simply can't take time off work every time your family member has an appointment, we go in your place. We keep you updated by WhatsApp at every step, so you can relax wherever you are.

Events and family visits

Company for what's worth sharing.

We accompany you to celebrations, family gatherings, church and religious services, or any occasion worth sharing in good company. We wait with you through the event and bring you home whenever you're ready.

Walks and social life

Sometimes what's needed is company.

Sometimes what's needed isn't an errand, but company: a walk along the seafront, a visit to a friend, or simply getting out of the house for a while with someone trustworthy by your side.
