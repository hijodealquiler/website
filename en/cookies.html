Cookie policy

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files this website may store in your browser to remember your preferences.

2. Cookies we use

This site uses a single, strictly necessary technical cookie that remembers whether you accepted or rejected the cookie notice, so it isn't shown again on your next visit. We do not use analytics cookies (we don't use Google Analytics or any equivalent tool), advertising cookies, or third-party cookies of any kind.

3. Managing cookies

You can accept or reject this cookie from the banner shown when you visit the site. If you reject it, you'll simply see the banner again on your next visit. You can also delete it at any time from your browser settings.

4. If we add analytics in the future

If we ever decide to use analytics or third-party tools, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before activating them.
